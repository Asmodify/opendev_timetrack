Automatic tracking notice

If you accept, the desktop helper on your computer records when you are working, so you don't have to clock in by hand.

  • What is recorded: start and end times of activity on your computer (whether the keyboard or mouse was used in the last few minutes), and whether you are on the office network or working remotely.
  • What is never recorded: keystrokes, screenshots, websites, apps, files or your exact GPS location.
  • Who can see it: you, your manager and HR/admins.
  • Your control: you can withdraw at any time on this page. You can always clock in and out by hand instead, and request corrections.
